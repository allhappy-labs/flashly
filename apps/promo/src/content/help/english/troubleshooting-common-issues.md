If something feels off, use these checks before reaching out.

## Sign in or account issues

- Confirm you are using the same email across web and mobile.
- Try signing out and back in to refresh your session.

## Sync problems

- Verify your internet connection on both devices.
- Open the deck list to trigger a refresh.

## Missing images or audio

- Wait a moment on slower connections so media can download.
- Reopen the deck to recheck the media.

## Import errors

- Preview your import to confirm the format before saving.
- Keep card text short and avoid extra separators.

If the issue continues, contact support with the deck name and device details.
