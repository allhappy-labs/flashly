Flashly offers multiple ways to study, so you can switch based on your goal.

## Study modes at a glance

- Classic: swipe-based grading with spaced repetition.
- Learn: choose goals, question types, and answer direction.
- Test: formal recall when you want to check readiness.
- Match: fast pairing for quick review.
- Write: typed recall for stronger memory.
- Browse: flip through cards with quick audio playback.

## How grading affects spacing

- Again, Hard, Good, and Easy adjust when a card returns.
- The better you recall, the longer the interval before the next review.

## Personal controls

- Set a daily goal to keep sessions manageable.
- Allow extra new cards when you want to move faster.
- Toggle haptics and auto-grading based on your preference.
