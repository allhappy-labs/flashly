Keep your account up to date and make sure purchases stay available.

## Profile settings

- Update your display name, username, and avatar.
- Use the same email to keep your web and mobile data connected.

## Billing basics

- Flashly uses one time payments rather than subscriptions.
- View invoices in your account settings when available.
- Restore past purchases if you switch devices.

If you cannot find an invoice or restore fails, contact support.
