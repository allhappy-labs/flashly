The web app turns study material into flashcards in minutes.

## Create a deck from notes

- Paste or upload study material.
- Choose a format such as Q and A or cloze.
- Review the generated cards before saving.

## Sync to mobile

- Saved decks sync to the mobile app.
- Keep studying on the go without reformatting your notes.

Tip: Use shorter sections of notes for cleaner, more focused cards.
