Flashly keeps decks tidy and flexible so you can study faster.

## Deck basics

- Create new decks with names, descriptions, and category accents.
- Edit deck details at any time.
- Sort decks by recent study or newest added.

## Card details that matter

- Use front and back fields for clear prompts and answers.
- Add images, audio, and example sentences when they help recall.
- Tag cards so you can filter and review later.
- Star important cards for focused practice.

Tip: Keep each card focused on a single idea to improve retention.
