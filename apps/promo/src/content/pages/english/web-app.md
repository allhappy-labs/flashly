## Flashly Web: Instant Flashcards

Paste notes, choose a format (Q&A or cloze), and generate cards in seconds.

### How It Works

1. Paste or upload your study material
2. Pick a format and options
3. Review, edit, and sync to mobile

### Advanced Features

- Multi-language support
- Bulk import/export
- Account sync

### Works with Mobile

Your web-generated decks sync to mobile so you can keep studying on the go.
