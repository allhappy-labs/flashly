## Flashly for Language Learning

Vocabulary sticks when you see it at the right time. Flashly keeps your language practice short, consistent, and focused so you can build fluency without burning out.

### Who it's for

- Beginners building a core vocabulary
- Intermediate learners refining grammar and usage
- Advanced learners expanding domain-specific terms

### Why language learners stick with Flashly

- **Daily momentum:** Short sessions that fit real life
- **Smarter repetition:** Review words and phrases before you forget them
- **Multiple formats:** Q&A, cloze, and examples for natural usage
- **Progress you feel:** Streaks and retention trends keep motivation high

### Language-friendly workflows

- **Vocabulary sprint:** Import word lists and drill them daily
- **Sentence builder:** Turn notes into cloze cards for grammar practice
- **Pronunciation practice:** Study with audio-supported cards and examples

### Features that make it easy

- AI card generation from notes and word lists
- Flexible study modes for recall and writing practice
- Spaced repetition tuned to your performance
- Progress tracking across all decks

### What you can expect

- Better recall of words and phrases in real conversation
- Consistent daily practice without overwhelm
- A system you can reuse for every new language goal
