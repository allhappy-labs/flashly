## Flashly for Exam Prep

Studying for midterms, finals, boards, or certifications? Flashly helps you build clean, exam-ready decks in minutes, then uses spaced repetition to keep your review focused and efficient.

### Who it's for

- Undergrads and grad students preparing for high-stakes exams
- Self-learners tackling standardized tests or professional certifications
- Anyone who needs to turn dense notes into reliable recall

### Why exam students pick Flashly

- **Speed to first deck:** Paste notes, pick a format, and get a clean deck instantly
- **Target weak spots:** Review what you're about to forget, not what you already know
- **Short, focused sessions:** Make progress even when time is tight
- **Visible momentum:** Streaks and retention trends keep you moving

### Exam-focused workflows

- **Last-minute cram:** Quickly generate Q&A cards and drill them in short bursts
- **Deep review:** Mix cloze deletions with explanations to strengthen understanding
- **Active recall:** Use writing and matching modes to lock in critical facts

### Features that drive results

- AI card generation from notes or PDFs
- Multiple study modes for different learning goals
- Spaced repetition tuned to your performance
- Progress and retention analytics

### What you can expect

- Faster setup and less time formatting cards
- More accurate recall on test day
- A repeatable study system for every exam
