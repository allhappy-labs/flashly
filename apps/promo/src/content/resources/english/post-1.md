Spaced repetition is effective because it reinforces memory right before you forget. By reviewing at expanding intervals, you strengthen recall without overstudying.

## Key takeaways

- Short, consistent sessions beat cramming
- Review timing matters more than total time
- Mix new and old cards to keep sessions engaging
